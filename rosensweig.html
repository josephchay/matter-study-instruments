Field Notes

Every spike is a vertex arguing with a magnet.

Each of the mesh's ~2,500 vertices asks one question every frame: does the field point outward through me? An inverse-square source term answers, a saturating response curve keeps the reply bounded, and a per-vertex spring settles it into a spike, a dimple, or nothing at all. It's loosely inspired by the Rosensweig instability seen in real ferrofluids — not a magnetohydrodynamic solver, just a field law a browser can afford.

The Dish

Droplets that remember they used to be liquid.

These aren't rigid balls. When two touch below a speed threshold, real Matter.js bodies coalesce into one, with radius conserved by area — r′ = √(r₁² + r₂²). Scatter reverses it.
