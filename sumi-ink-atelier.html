study 01 / 04

Released from the word, the grains ride a divergence-free velocity field — the 2-D curl of simplex noise, ∇×ψ — so the ink swirls without ever compressing.

Your pointer is a wet brush. Its velocity is injected straight into the field as an impulse with a Gaussian falloff, plus a perpendicular swirl term, so a fast flick leaves a vortex street behind it. Press and hold anywhere and a spring force gathers the pigment to your fingertip; let go and it detonates outward. Each frame the trail buffer fades a few percent toward paper — the physics of a drying stroke.

integrator · semi-implicit euler on the gpu — one texel per grain
field · F = (∂ψ/∂y, −∂ψ/∂x) + brush + springs, damped per-frame
memory · ping-pong render targets, ink trail = exponential decay to paper

Three brushes hang by Verlet ropes — position-based dynamics, twelve constraint passes per frame, so the strands are inextensible but the bobs carry real momentum.

The heaviest bob swings slowest and hits hardest. Grab one, fling it, and watch the rope crack like a whip; a breath of simplex wind keeps them from ever quite resting.
