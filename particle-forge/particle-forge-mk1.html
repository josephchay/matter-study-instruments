CH.1 — FORMATIONS

Every shape is just a target.

Each particle is a damped harmonic oscillator chasing a position that isn't fixed. Scroll, and the target slides from a Fibonacci sphere to a torus traced with an irrational step sequence — the spring does the rest.

CH.2 — TURBULENCE

Order dissolves into flow.

A hashed 3D noise field perturbs every particle, and a cross-product term bends that noise into rotation — a cheap, curl-inspired stand-in for a divergence-free flow field. Keep scrolling into the drift.

a ≈ swirl(∇n) − c·v
CH.3 — SOFT BODIES

Drag it. Watch it wobble back.

These blobs aren't sprites — each is a ring of ten circular bodies held together by real Matter.js constraints acting as springs, plus a soft anchor to a hidden centre so they keep their shape. Grab an edge and let go.
